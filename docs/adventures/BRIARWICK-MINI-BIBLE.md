# Briarwick: mini voice bible (DRAFT)

Scope: the original Monster of the Week mystery *The Last Laugh of Briarwick*. Not the Amnesty fan-run sheet and not Josh's own campaign notes (Alphie, Marcus, Sace, Marrow, Vesper, Miros are real table characters and are never touched).

## Audit (2026-11-13)
- The scenario's cast already fits one register, a gothic-comic village: Torvin Pike, Orren Pike (one family, one surname), Milo Reed, Magistrate Honk, the Morrowmummer, the Mirthwake. Keep them.
- **One LLM-default name:** the central ghost **Mara Vell** (27 mentions in the Keeper doc). I did **not** rename her: the document is prep for a specific table, and I can't tell whether it has already been run. A rename would desync the Keeper's notes from anything the players heard.
- If you want her renamed, a draw from the generator (surname kept as *Vell* so the family link survives) gave: **Sahar Vell**, Lotad Vell, Deros Vell. Tell me and I'll apply it with a single replace and a diff for you to read.

## Voice
Warm, wry and dread-adjacent: jokes land, then the joke is the threat. Short stage-direction sentences. People are tradespeople and officials with small vanities. Every NPC gets a want that is not "survive the festival", a limit, and something they will not discuss.

`Marcus`, `Wren` and `Stonebrook` hits are table characters or rules text, not scenario names; left alone. Slop lint on the React components is not meaningful (class names and JSX strings score as prose), so no edits were made there.
