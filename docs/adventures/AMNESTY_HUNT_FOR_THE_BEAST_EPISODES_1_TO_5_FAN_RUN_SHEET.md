## FIRST PAGE — HOW TO RUN THIS TONIGHT

**Important distinction:** The original adventure is **modern-day Kepler, West Virginia**, surrounded by Monongahela National Forest. Do *not* reskin it to medieval dark fantasy if the request is for the original events exactly. Its comedic horror already has cryptids, mysterious gates and fire-wielding magic.

**Best three roles** (swap players if they want): Dylan plays **Duck Newton** (*The Chosen*, anxious park ranger with a destined talking sword called Beacon); Gilbert plays **Aubrey Little** (*The Spell-Slinger*, stage magician whose fire is real; brings rabbit Dr Harris Bonkers); Josh plays **Ned Chicane** (*The Crooked*, desperate operator of the Cryptonomica and an enthusiastic faker of Bigfoot evidence). These exact playbooks are confirmed in the original setup. Use Evil Hat's official free character sheets, not homebrew copies. [CANON character mappings; TABLE ADAPTATION player assignments]

**Non-negotiable plot landmarks (not forced actions):**
1. Pidgeon reports the unnatural bear that attacked her and Pete; some evidence suggests **fire** drove it away.
2. Duck tracks it in the woods while visions of **Minerva** urge him to face destiny; Aubrey's stage act attracts **Mama**; Ned tries to save the **Cryptonomica** through a fraudulent Bigfoot stunt.
3. **Amnesty Lodge** reveals friendly supernatural beings; Ned witnesses cook **Barclay turn into Bigfoot**; Duck crosses the mysterious stone arch to **Sylvain** and encounters **Vincent**.
4. The hunters converge at the gateway; the monstrous patchwork Beast and black-ooze-controlled animals attack; fire works, but the threat survives and withdraws.
5. **Mama**, the Pine Guard's leader, tells the hunters what an **Abomination** is; the party learns its behaviours, especially absorbing animals, its attraction to animal scent, and susceptibility to fire.
6. The group makes a plan: lure the Beast to **Crooked Bend Cave**; in the podcast Ned uses his animal-scented Wookiee costume and his Lincoln, Duck retrieves **Beacon**, and Aubrey positions a fuel-and-fire trap.
7. The final fight reveals a black, lizard-like inner creature beneath the fused patchwork skin. The combination of fire, teamwork and Duck's weapon brings down the Beast, and the heroes begin their new roles with the Pine Guard.

**Not possible to fix exactly:** The actual players' dice rolls, joke choices, fire placement, creature positioning and last attack are emergent play. To be faithful to *MotW*, preserve the **situation** and creature weakness, but permit different choices. A precisely re-enacted battle would be an acted transcript, not an RPG session.

**Table safety:** Strange body-horror, animal predation, weapons, dangerous fire, menace, criminal backstories. Ask everyone privately/briefly what to exclude or fade to black.

## EXACTLY WHAT TO DO AT THE TABLE (ORIGINAL CONNECTIVE NARRATION, NOT PODCAST TEXT)

### A. 17:20 — EPISODE 1: THREE COLD OPENINGS
**[CANON]** Kepler, West Virginia. Three heroes still mostly strangers. 

**Duck's scene (12–15m):**
- Say in your own words: “Duck, the ranger office sends you to a campsite near Lot 5. The camper's fire should not be burning there, and someone appears to be missing.”
- **Pidgeon** reports that she and Pete encountered an immense patchwork bear; Pete is missing. The monster retreated when it approached her fire. She is distressed, not dishonest.
- Let Duck ask questions/inspect tracks. Show **black slime**, snapped vegetation, an enormous shape with features of several animals.
- Aim to finish with Duck heading deeper into the forest; don't force the hunter to ignore a danger.

**Aubrey's scene (10–15m):**
- Say: “Aubrey, the audience at your low-paid magic show barely notices you. Onstage, your trick becomes frighteningly more real than you meant.”
- Mama notices actual fire magic, gets involved and offers a ride to Kepler / the Amnesty Lodge. Dr Harris Bonkers can be a harmless comic release.
- Aim to finish with Mama's invitation. Preserve agency if Aubrey refuses: Mama can reveal information via a second contact, or the player can later follow a clue.

**Ned's scene (10–15m):**
- Say: “Ned, the Cryptonomica's latest bill is one you genuinely cannot afford. What ridiculous proof of Bigfoot will you film to attract customers?”
- Actual original scheme: a **Wookiee outfit**, giant false tracks, filming equipment, and revolting animal-scented material; the original also involves an eviction warning.
- Give one delightfully embarrassing roll or social consequence but **do not** let the elaborate fraud consume 30 minutes.
- Aim to get him into the woods to encounter Barclay later.

**If the players aren't using original characters:** Give these three prompts based on a ranger, fire-mage and crook-like cryptid expert, but label that an *alternate-casting adaptation*; their personal plot lines won't be literally identical.

### B. 18:05 — EPISODE 2: THE TRUTH RISES
**Duck:** He finds scorched, animal-shaped marks, an unnaturally silent section of forest and the dropped flashlight. **Minerva** (a luminous mentor figure in his visions) calls him towards his Chosen destiny. The patchwork Beast finds him. Provide credible flight routes including the moonlit **stone archway**, which he can choose to cross.

**Aubrey:** **Mama** takes her to **Amnesty Lodge**; **Dani**, **Jake Coolice** and other lodgers make the place hospitable, quirky and uncanny. When Aubrey demonstrates genuine fire, Mama reveals that magic exists and friendly visitors from another world live nearby. Make it playful *without* treating Sylphs as monsters to shoot.

**Ned:** Staging a Bigfoot film, he catches **Barclay** handling a strange crystal. A sickly **bobcat** controlled by black slime appears; Barclay reveals his **Bigfoot** form to protect Ned. Permit flight; goal is proof of a separate supernatural threat and the link with Lodge.

**Clue card (give plainly if asked):**
- Fire scared the original attacker's quarry away.
- Multiple infected animals behave as **pieces of one threat**.
- Mama and Barclay know more than they first admit.
- The unmarked archway is dangerous/important, not an ordinary ruin.

### C. 18:55 — EPISODE 3: CROSS THE BORDER, MEET THE PARTY
**Duck:** Crossing the arch leads briefly to **Sylvain**, an otherworldly place; he is questioned by **Vincent**, who is concerned about crossings and protecting the secrecy/pact. In a compressed version, 5–8 minutes establishes otherworldly reality, guarded access, then returns Duck. Don't reveal later-season cosmology.

**Ned + Barclay:** They drive toward the clearing to warn Mama about the corrupted animals. The car and its driver may create an absurd complication. 

**Aubrey + Mama:** Come to the gate as crises converge. All three hunters finally have a reason to work together.

**First Beast battle:** Show beast as an enormous stitched-together animal with black slime, several eyes and absorbed creatures. Allow a brief defence/rescue. **Fire injures it; a storm douses flames; Mama and Barclay's arrival and/or the party's actions force it back into the forest.** Do not run this as an hour-long boss fight. Five to ten useful exchanges is plenty, with Keeper moves as appropriate.

**If the team kills it early:** Celebrate the unexpected success instead of faking its resurrection. Bring Ep 4's revelation/aftermath forward and have another short challenge only if they want it. Fidelity to the original story yields to actual player agency.

### D. 19:35 — EPISODE 4: AMNESTY LODGE BRIEFING
**[CANON]** Mama heads the **Pine Guard**, hunters who protect Kepler from hostile **Abominations**, while peaceful **Sylphs** may live as Lodge residents. Abominations arrive near the gate periodically and initially remain near it, giving a window to hunt them.
1. Mama states the danger: the Beast must be tracked before it roams beyond the woods.
2. Ask each player what evidence they observed, then confirm: **absorbs animals**, corrupts them with **black ooze**, **responds to strong animal scents**, and **fire** hurts it.
3. Mama produces a forest map and asks where they'd choose a trap. **Crooked Bend Cave** is an accessible, believable option; do not forbid another good site.
4. Ask how they'll attract and trap the creature. In the recording **Ned proposes driving in his Wookiee costume coated with animal stink** and **Aubrey plans an oil-based fire trap**. Let the players own the plan if they suggest alternatives.
5. Remind Duck that his strange stored weapon is still at the Cryptonomica. **Beacon** is a talking, razor-tongued destiny sword; if the hunter chooses to retrieve it, give a quick fun interaction, not 20 minutes of exposition.

**Recommended scene prompts:** “What did your hunter learn in the clearing?” “What part of the map would let you control this thing?” “Which risk are you actually willing to take to save Kepler?”

### E. 20:10 — EPISODE 5: THE CAVE HUNT
**[CANON]** Ned acts as lure with his car and strange costume; the others prepare a fire trap in Crooked Bend Cave. The Beast initially wears a mantle of fused animal hide; the final reveal is a black lizard-like creature of writhing slime with a sharp tail beneath it. Fire is decisive, but teamwork with Beacon matters.

**[TABLE ADAPTATION]** Simple staged threat:
- **Chase, 10–20m:** Whoever is bait drives/decoys through the woods while the Beast keeps pace. Choose a risk: falling tree, snapped brake line, or Beast's lunge. Resolve 1–2 meaningful rolls, not a ten-roll chase minigame.
- **Set the trap, 10m:** Other hunters prepare, protect one safe exit, decide where fire can be controlled. Their plan should be effective if plausible. No need to reproduce the identical oil-ring configuration.
- **Stage 1 showdown, 15–25m:** The monster strikes, a burning trap changes its behaviour, some animals detach or rush to its aid, the heroes see it fears flame.
- **Stage 2 reveal, 10–20m:** Damaged patchwork fur sloughs away: the inner lizard-like black creature lunges desperately. Let the group exploit its weaknesses with teamwork, magical fire and fighting.
- **Finish, 5–10m:** On a meaningful fatal blow while it is exposed to its weakness, it bursts into black mist or collapses to ash. Play out the hunters' individual consequences, then cut to dawn.

**Homebrew convenience stats — NOT established as Griffin's original stat block:** Beast **10 harm capacity, 2 armour**, claws **3-harm intimate/hand**, violent collision **2-harm close**, patchwork regeneration *unless its fire weakness is exploited*; fire suppresses regeneration and is necessary for a lasting kill. Controlled animals: **3 harm, 0 armour**, bite/claw **1-harm hand**. These are **optional suggested table numbers**, not a recovered original source; adjust to actual hunters' harm and the tone, and run strictly by the official rules for harm and armour.

**If a hunter doesn't have fire magic:** Mama can bring a controlled flame or improvised fire source. The Beast's weakness must be solvable, not gated behind Gilbert selecting exactly one power.

**If everyone dies/retreats or fire is impossible:** Allow a rescue, costly withdrawal or negotiated reset if it follows the fiction. Do not narrate pre-ordained success or invent a death to mimic the podcast.

## WHAT TO SAY WHEN YOU GET STUCK (ORIGINAL KEEPER PROMPTS)

Use these **original, non-canonical prompts**; they are not podcast dialogue:

- Duck: “The forest has gone absolutely quiet. There should be birds, insects, anything. Your torch catches a lump of black residue on a branch. What do you do?”
- Aubrey: “Nobody at this lodge seems shocked by the fire you made. The strange thing is that they look relieved. Who do you question first?”
- Ned: “That big, shaggy man just became something the Cryptonomica would pay a fortune to photograph. Then you hear a second creature behind you. What do you do?”
- Convergence: “You reach a clearing lit by the moon. Three strangers are looking at a stone gateway as something huge crashes through the trees. Who reacts first?”
- Mama's plan: “You've got one night, a forest map, and one clear weakness. How are you going to make it chase you somewhere you control?”
- Cave: “The creature's hide writhes with limbs that don't belong together. A hot, oily smell rises from the ground. It's almost where you wanted it. What do you do?”
- Aftermath: “Kepler is quiet again. What part of this night will your hunter never quite be able to explain to anyone else?”

## REAL GAMEPLAY REMINDERS FOR A FIRST-TIME KEEPER

- The **Keeper never rolls the monster's attack**; resolve hunter moves and the fiction.
- Roll only for moves triggered by action. Use official move text for 10+, 7–9 and misses; don't impose your own generic result in place of its prescribed choices.
- For *Investigate a Mystery*, give valid information even when clues are uncomfortable to face. For a failed roll, you can reveal the clue with a dangerous complication instead of dead-ending the story.
- Give Duck, Aubrey and Ned **one important decision** each in the first hour. Their separate introductions only work if the spotlight rotates.
- Keep an accessible notecard with the Beast's 3 critical traits: **patchwork animal body**, **black ooze/animal control**, **fire vulnerability**.
- Distinguish **knowing fire damages it** from **choosing a safe trap**, which belongs to the players.
- Do not demand that Ned act like Clint, Aubrey like Travis or Duck like Justin. Their characters needn't repeat original jokes.
- If player improvisation replaces the Wookiee lure or cave with an equally clever plan, accept it even though the exact episode changes.
- If you're trying for the shortest session, remove flashbacks first; **never cut the monster's weakness, the Lodge revelation or the final confrontation**.
- Don't share the spoilers in this page with hunters.
